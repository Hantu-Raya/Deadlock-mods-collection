Detailed Postgame turns the post-game Scoreboard screen into a stats board for all 12 players. It shows every stat of the match on one screen: exact numbers, where each player's souls came from, final items, and a soul lead graph with the objectives marked on it. It appears whenever the game opens a match on the post-game page.

Exact numbers
  • Full values, not rounded: souls, kills, deaths, assists, player damage, objective damage and healing for every player.
  • The best value in the match for each stat is highlighted.
  • MVP and Key Player slips sit on the hero portraits.
  • Team net worth, kills, game time and match ID are in the header.
Souls by source and final items
  • Each player's souls split by source, with percentages: troopers, neutrals, breakables, team bonus, enemy kills, objectives, urn, assists and denies.
  • Each player's final items sit below.
Soul lead graph
  • Shows which team was ahead, and by how much, over the whole match.
  • Guardians, Walkers and base Guardians are marked on the timeline in the colour of the team that took them.
  • Both teams' peak leads and the final lead are labelled.
Other details
  • Click the match ID in the header to copy it.
  • Fits 4:3 screens: the board scales down so nothing is cut off.
  • The board reads everything from the game's own post-game page. It makes no web requests.
Install
  1. Put pak11_dir.vpk in Deadlock\game\citadel\addons. Create the addons folder if it is missing.
  2. If you have never used mods: open game\citadel\gameinfo.gi and add the line Game citadel/addons above Game citadel in SearchPaths.
  3. Restart Deadlock and open a match on the post-game page, for example from your match history. The board is on the Scoreboard screen of the post-game page.
If you already have a pak11_dir.vpk from another mod, rename this one to a free number.

Works with other mods
  • Only one mod can own the post-game page. Observer HUD and DLF ship their own version of this board, so with either installed, theirs shows instead of this one.
  • Works alongside ShowRank.
Known limits
  • The graph, the objective markers and souls by source take a moment to appear after the board opens, because the board reads them from the game's Graphs screen.
  • The mid boss, the shrines, the patron and one of the Walkers are not on the timeline. The game's post-game page does not list them.
  • A player with more than 15 final items overlaps the top edge of the graph.
