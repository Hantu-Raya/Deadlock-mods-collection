# Detailed Postgame v1
Turns the post-game **Scoreboard** screen into a stats board for all 12 players. Every stat of the match on one screen.

**What it shows**
- **Exact numbers**, not rounded: souls, kills, deaths, assists, player damage, objective damage and healing. The best value in the match is highlighted.
- **Souls by source** for every player: troopers, neutrals, breakables, team bonus, enemy kills, objectives, urn, assists and denies.
- **Final items**, plus MVP and Key Player slips.
- **Soul lead graph**: who was ahead and by how much, with objectives marked in the colour of the team that took them.
- Click the **match ID** to copy it.
- Fits **4:3** screens.

**Install**
1. Put `pak11_dir.vpk` in `Deadlock\game\citadel\addons`.
2. Restart Deadlock.
3. Open a match on the post-game page and go to the Scoreboard screen.

**Notes**
- Only one mod can own the post-game page. With Observer HUD or DLF installed, their board shows instead.
- Works alongside ShowRank.
- Reads everything from the game's own post-game page. No web requests.
