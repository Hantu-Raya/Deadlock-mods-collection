## Installation

1. Download your preferred `.vpk` file
2. Copy to your Deadlock addons folder:
   ```
   Steam\steamapps\common\dota 2 beta\game\dota_addons\
   ```
3. Launch Deadlock and enjoy!

## Finding Your Perfect Position

Not sure which to pick? Try these common positions:

- **-3% to -6%**: Higher visibility, good for large monitors
- **0%**: Stock game position
- **10% to 15%**: Lower center, popular for ultrawide setups
- **20% to 25%**: Bottom of screen, minimal HUD interference
