### Smoke scenarios

1. Spectate a match or play a demo: banner text fits and aligns, bans cover their boxes, the top-bar offset is right, rings appear after 5:00, ult counters tick.
2. ESC, DLF BROADCAST: edit every field on every page, close and reopen, restart the game: the edits persist. Turn DEV icons OFF and ON.
3. Hero testing with an enemy bot and STATUS LOG on: land each listed ultimate; the top bar shows it (stuns with the ring), the healthbar shows no stun icon, and the icon fades out after the status ends. Shoulder Charge and unlisted abilities show nothing on the top bar.
4. To see which modifier an ability really applies: `cl_modifier_dump <entity index>` (index from `Created bot <index>/hero_...`) prints `[n] <vdata name> (<class>) (<seconds left>)`. Never `cl_modifier_dump_visible`; it crashes the client. `cl_modifier_dump_list` only lists registered names.
5. Soul graph (new, 2026-10-23, proposed): on a demo, ESC, SOUL GRAPH, change a setting: the graph previews for 8 s (PREVIEW sample data before two pins); play past a mark: it shows for the chosen time; seek back: later samples are dropped; open the scoreboard and the shop: it hides.
6. Spectator VData (new, 2026-10-23, proposed): watch a hero hooked by Bebop; the watched hero's HUD must not print `#Citadel_Ability_Hook/...`.
