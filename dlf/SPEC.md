# DLF observer HUD: behavior

What the pak shows and does. Terms are in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md); geometry and the build contract are in [design.md](design.md); engine facts are in [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md); what has been seen live is in [LIVE-VERIFIED.md](LIVE-VERIFIED.md). Nothing here is a live claim unless it says so.

## Goal

A spectator HUD for the DLF league, installed on the observer PC only (not players' PCs, and independent of HPv2's pak02): the Deadlock Observer Mod (QOLLOCK spectator timers, soul difference, per-player purchase notices) rebased on current stock, plus a DLF broadcast banner, a soul lead graph, pickup rings, ultimate cooldowns and debuff icons on the top bar. It ships as one `pak01_dir.vpk` and the banner fonts in a 7z laid out like the original `Deadlock_Observer_Mod_26.07.28.zip` (a `game` folder to drag onto the Deadlock folder). The 7z has no `gameinfo.gi` (since 2026-10-20): the observer PC's `citadel/gameinfo.gi` must already mount `citadel/addons` (`Game citadel/addons` before `Game citadel`, as in the repo's reference `dlf/gameinfo/gameinfo.gi`; [ADR 0006](docs/adr/0006-release-archive-without-gameinfo.md)).

## Banner

- A 1920x100 banner above the top bar (a 68px band with a torn bottom edge that fades behind the cards, from `G:\NEW HUD 3 ban.psd`, 2026-10-28; `G:\NEW HUD(1).psd` 2026-10-20 before it): `SEASON n` / `MATCH #n`, series format, both team names and scores, three hero bans per team (two until 2026-10-28), a logo per team, and the twelve player names (six per team). It starts at the screen's top edge when spectating too (stock moves the spectator top bar 8px down; DLF cancels that).
- Team name and score follow the team (Amber/Sapphire), not the screen side. Logos and emblems follow the team too.
- Each text section sits in a fixed band inside its frame in the art. Long text gets a smaller whole font size (and, if needed, no letter spacing) instead of the engine's shrink, which warped the stencil font; it never runs out of the frame. Team names sit at the band's outer ends (2026-10-28 art): Amber's starts at the left frame, Sapphire's ends at the right one, so the TEAM NAMES X offset is limited to ±6 px (it was ±40; a saved value outside that falls back to 0).
- Each player name sits under its own top-bar card, wherever the stock bar packs the cards (short teams, hero-testing order); empty slots show nothing. Names come from each card's `.AlwaysPlayerName` (falling back to `.PlayerName`), or the slot's alias from the editor. The loop runs at 1 Hz, rescans the roster every 5 s or when a cached panel dies, and writes only on change. It validates only `#DLFBanner` per tick: every panel it writes is a descendant.
- Banner text uses Modesto Lite Expanded (the supplied face for the 2026-10-20 PSD; the PSD's player and score text was Modesto Poster, which was not in the zip). Without it the banner falls back to GOFIRE. The ult counter and debuff seconds and counts keep the game's Jost. GOFIRE and Operation Napalm (CC0) are selectable in the editor and, like Modesto, install as loose files ([ADR 0003](docs/adr/0003-fonts-as-loose-renamed-files.md)); without them the banner falls back to Jost. Modesto Lite Expanded is an Adobe Typekit font (check its licence before publishing the archive). GOFIRE is "free for personal use" (1001fonts, Sealoung): a public or monetized broadcast needs the author's permission.
- Bans: three per team (user 2026-10-28, from the new PSD); a ban portrait covers the matching box in the art, slot 1 leftmost; an empty ban leaves the box. One ban per hero: picking a hero already banned moves it. Hero IDs missing from `dlf_heroes.js` (removed by a patch) become empty slots. A save, share code or `match.json` with two bans gets an empty third slot.
- Team logos: each team's faction emblem by default (Amber the Hidden King, Sapphire the Archmother, from the PSD); the DLF logo sits in the art between the scores. A web image URL (http/https; PNG, JPEG, still WebP or GIF) replaces the emblem once it loads, at 50-200% (height capped by the banner). The game draws animated WebP as grey bars and does not load video, so links from image and GIF hosts are turned into the file itself (`$.DLFState.logoSources`, Observer HUD's list, 2026-10-24): imgur page and `.gifv`/`.mp4`/`.webm`/`.webp` links load the GIF, then the still image; Giphy and Tenor links their GIF; Discord media-proxy and fxtwitter `.webp` as GIF; Discord CDN emoji, avatars and icons as GIF when animated, else PNG; X's WebP as PNG; GitHub blob pages raw; Dropbox share pages with `raw=1`. The editor keeps the URL as entered. Other animated WebP links do not work. A URL whose every source fails keeps the emblem and says FAILED in the editor.
- Logo mechanics: `dlf_banner.js` creates one `Image` per source and shows it only after its own `ImageLoaded`; a failed source is deleted and the next one tried; a replaced image is deleted, and the team's emblem stays until one loads. It publishes `{ amber/sapphire: default|loading|loaded|failed }` on `dlf_logo_status` for the editor. URLs are validated by `$.DLFState.cleanUrl` (http/https, plain host, no credentials, spaces, controls or backslashes, at most 512 characters; rejected, not truncated) and never logged.
- Style: fonts, sizes, letter spacing and offsets are written only when `style` changes. Limits per section (`STYLE_LIMITS` in `dlf_state.js`) keep the text in its frame.
- Per-player purchase notices sit right under each card's status grid, 2 px under the ult cooldown counter when the card shows no status (as Observer HUD, 2026-10-24: 169 px down the card, plus 39 px per grid row); they move when statuses come and go. The recent-purchase feature was originally made by **bytenode** (https://gamebanana.com/members/5222690); since 2026-10-24 DLF runs Observer HUD's rewrite (`dlf_buys.js`) instead of the decompiled QOLLOCK copy, and since the same day (user: "add wobble recent purchase from the observer hud") Observer HUD's notice look too: one line in a frame at most 205 px wide, the stock shop card's paper (slot/tier backer, wear, grain) under a frame drawn as three anti-aliased images (`images/dlf/buy_frame2_*`, `buy_frame3_*` for tier 4, copied from Observer HUD), in a layer (`.DLFBuyBg`) that wobbles with the stock chat bubble's poses at 35 % (4 s loop, 400 ms frames); the icon and the name (stock "Spotted" ping font, black, tier 4 in light slot colours) stay still. A CSS border on the wobbling layer stair-stepped live in Observer HUD, hence the images. QOLLOCK's look (flat backer, CSS border, 11 px name) was used until then. The host is QOLLOCK's `.QuickPurchasesPanel` with `.DLFBuyHost`, a fixed 209 px (Observer HUD's column width): in QOLLOCK's fit-children host the notice kept a stale, narrower width live (user screenshots 2026-10-24, "render half": the frame ended inside "Express Sh|ot", the paper short of the right cap). Notices centre in the host. Stack widths for the overlap rule come from the widest notice, not the host. It reads the stock shop list `#RecentPurchasesContainer` without overriding the shop layout: one child count every 0.5 s, rows read once when they appear. A purchase goes to the card on the buyer's team (`isTeam1Purchase` Amber, `isTeam2Purchase` Sapphire) with the same hero id (row `#RecentPurchaseHeroImage` against the card's `#HeroBadge`), else the same hero name (a hidden label of ours bound to the row's `{s:recent_hero_name}`); no match or two matches draw nothing. Rows already in the list at first sight (a mid-game join) and rows stamped more than 10 s before the game clock (a seek or a refill) never show. Three at most per card, newest on top, each for `state.buys` seconds (default 5, set on the DEV page), then a 0.4 s fade. Every stack is centred on its own card, the innermost too, as Observer HUD (user 2026-10-24, screenshot `20261024193435_1.jpg`: Infernus's notice, aligned to his card's inner edge, reached over the next card; the inner-edge alignment `.DLFBuyHugRight` / `.DLFBuyHugLeft` from the same day's review is removed). A wide notice on an innermost card can reach a little into the clock column. Neighbouring stacks of a team are laid out by their screen x: a stack that would overlap an earlier one moves 4 px under it. A card or debuff column that was never laid out (Infinity) keeps the natural top. Item icons come from `MOD_ICONS` (English names refreshed from the game data by `refresh-stock.py`, other languages as QOLLOCK shipped them); a renamed or new item shows its name without an icon. The shop's own recent-purchases panel stays hidden by DLF's `citadel_hud_hero_shop.css` override.
- Purchase notice time (2026-10-24, as Observer HUD's `state.buys`): `state.buys` is 0 (OFF), 3, 5, 8 or 15 seconds, default 5 (it was a fixed 5 s in `dlf_buys.js`). The editor's DEV page has one choice row (PURCHASE NOTICE SHOWS FOR); `dlf_buys.js` reads the value from `dlf_match_state` when a notice is about to show (not per tick, so the idle cost is unchanged) and 0 shows no notice. It saves with the match, travels in share codes, and `match.json` may set it (`buys`, checked by `write-match.js`).
- Kill feed (2026-10-24, Observer HUD's `placeFeed`; same stock panel, user screenshot 2026-10-23): the stock `#EventFeed` (top left, margin-top 200) could cover the leftmost cards' status grid, ult counter and purchase notices. While it holds a kill row, `dlf_buys.js` moves it down (inline margin-top, whole px) to 6 px under the lowest of those whose left edge is left of the feed's right edge (its widest row, else 360 px): a card's `#DLFDebuffs` while it has rows, its `#DLFUltCooldown.DLFCounting` (167 px under the card's top), its notice stack. It goes down at once and back up only after 2 s, so a blinking status does not bounce it; back at the stock place it is written as `200px`, never cleared. An empty feed is not moved and is looked at once a second; while it has rows or sits moved, every 0.5 s poll. Card places are cached for 30 s (2 s while a card is not laid out); panels that read Infinity are skipped. A reload finds the feed where the last instance left it (the inline margin) and works out the stock place from it. Not seen live.
- The stock net/FPS stats (`HudPerfStatsBasics`, top right, shown when the player turns them on) start 100 px down, under the banner, instead of 8 px (`styles/hud.css`, 2026-10-24); they covered BEST OF 3. Not live-verified that the hud.xml stylesheet reaches that panel.

## Match state

`{ season, match, seriesFormat, banCount (2|3, default 3), amber/sapphire: { name, score, bans: [heroId, heroId, heroId], logo: { url, scale } }, style: { event|team|player|score|series: { font, size, x, y, spacing } }, dev: { debuffs, debuffPollMs, logStatus }, graph: { show, first, every, size, x, y, line, fill }, buys, cues: { bridge, urn, rift, mid }, autoSeries, aliases }`.

- Everything goes through `$.DLFState.normalize`: missing or out-of-range fields take the `match.json` defaults field by field. `write-match.js` is the strict build-time check of the same schema.
- The editor publishes every edit at once on the HUD root attribute `dlf_match_state`. The banner, the debuff column and the soul graph re-read it each 1 s tick. On boot the editor adopts an existing attribute (an ESC layout reload) instead of the saved record.
- Other attributes: `dlf_roster` (live game names per team slot, written by the banner), `dlf_logo_status`, `dlf_player_names` (session aliases) and `dlf_accounts` (game name to account id).

## ESC menu editor

A DLF BROADCAST button in the Escape menu (between SWAP HERO and PLAYER FEEDBACK; SWAP HERO moved up by one row, it overlapped the button) opens the editor: a header (title, save status, RESET <page>, RESET ALL, CLOSE), a tab strip and one fixed-height page area (352 px), so the tabs and the shell never move while a page changes ([ADR 0004](docs/adr/0004-editor-as-tabbed-pages.md)). The editor opens on MATCH, keeps the page while it is closed and reopened in a session, and starts on MATCH again after a layout reload. Every edit shows on the banner at once. The hero picker takes the page area's place and gives the page back when it closes.

Escape handling: clicks on the editor's empty space or the dimmed background do nothing (they no longer reach the stock menu), and ESC closes the hero picker, then the editor, then resumes the game. The ESC root `oncancel`, `#EscapeBackground` and `#EscapeButton` call `$.DLFMenuCancel()` first and resume only when it returns false.

Pages, in tab order:

- **MATCH**: season, match, series format, AUTO SERIES (ON/OFF, default OFF; see [Series from the match end](#series-from-the-match-end)), BANS (3 or 2 per team, one click flips it; at 2 the third ban button collapses and its ban is kept for a switch back, and the banner shows `dlf_banner_2ban.png`, the art with the inner box of each side painted out, with the right side's two bans on its outer boxes), then per team side by side the series score and the bans (the column titles show the team names). SEASON and MATCH take a number in the box (0-9999, a leading # is fine; Enter or leaving the box commits, Escape reverts, other text puts the current number back) or - and + steppers.
- **TEAMS**: per team the name, the logo URL (Enter commits, Escape reverts; an invalid URL is rejected and the logo stays), the load state (TEAM EMBLEM, LOADING, WEB LOGO, FAILED) and the size stepper (10% steps).
- **PLAYERS**: see below.
- **SOUL GRAPH**: edits `state.graph` ([Soul graph](#soul-graph)). SHOW AT EACH MARK (one-click OFF, 15 S, 30 S, 60 S, ALWAYS; default 30 S), then a second row with FIRST MARK AT (2, 5, 10, 15, 20 MIN; default 10 MIN) and THEN EVERY (1, 2, 3, 5, 10 MIN; default 5 MIN; user 2026-10-24: first show at 10:00, then every 5:00, both editable; until then MARKS EVERY alone, marks on its multiples from 5:00), SIZE (70-150%, 10% steps), X (-600..600 px, 10 px steps; ±300 until 2026-10-24, Observer HUD's range), Y (-40..300 px, 5 px steps; 200 until then; default 35 px since 2026-10-24, 0 before, so the graph sits lower; a saved record keeps its own Y until RESET), LINE (GREEN, WHITE, GOLD, CYAN), AREA FILL ON/OFF and a RESET that restores the graph defaults only (no confirmation). The choices and limits come from `GRAPH_SHOW`, `GRAPH_FIRST`, `GRAPH_EVERY`, `GRAPH_LIMITS`, `GRAPH_LINES` and `GRAPH_DEFAULTS` in `dlf_state.js`; the choice buttons are built from those tables. A save from before has no `first` and gets 10 MIN.
- **SOUL GRAPH, OBJECTIVE TIMERS** (2026-10-24, Observer HUD's cues): how early each top-bar timer shows, `state.cues` ([Objective timers](#objective-timers)). BRIDGE BUFFS, URN and MID-BOSS: OFF, 15 S, 30 S, 60 S, 120 S, ALWAYS (`CUE_SHOW`); RIFT: OFF or ON (`RIFT_SHOW`, its only time is the 20 s warning). Defaults: bridge, mid-boss and rift ALWAYS (as before), urn 30 S. The header's RESET SOUL GRAPH puts these back too; the graph's own RESET does not.
- **STYLE**: BANNER TEXT STYLE tabs for SEASON / MATCH, TEAM NAMES, PLAYER NAMES, SCORES and SERIES FORMAT, each with FONT (MODESTO, GOFIRE, NAPALM, JOST), MAX SIZE, X, Y and SPACING steppers and RESET.
- **SHARE / SAVE**: SHARE SETTINGS, then THIS PC'S SAVE with FORGET SAVE set apart (RESET moved to the header, 2026-10-24). See [Save and share](#save-and-share).
- **DEV** (right end of the strip): debuff icons ON/OFF, the world-bar poll interval (0.1-2 s, default 0.25 s) and STATUS LOG (default OFF), then the PURCHASE NOTICE SHOWS FOR choices (OFF, 3, 5, 8, 15 S). They save with the match; RESET DEV restores them.
- **RESET** (header, left of CLOSE; Observer HUD's two-step reset, 2026-10-24): RESET ALL and RESET <current page> (MATCH, TEAMS, PLAYERS, SOUL GRAPH, STYLE or DEV; hidden on SHARE / SAVE, which has nothing of its own). The first press only asks (the label reads AGAIN TO RESET ALL / AGAIN TO RESET <PAGE>); a second press within 4 s acts. The question lapses after 4 s, on a page change, when the editor closes, and pressing the other button asks that one instead. RESET ALL puts every setting back, DEV and the notice time included, and repairs a damaged or newer save as the old single-click RESET TO DEFAULTS did. RESET <page>: MATCH season, match, series format, scores and bans; TEAMS names and logos; PLAYERS aliases (saved and session); SOUL GRAPH the graph; STYLE every banner text section; DEV the DEV settings and the notice time. Defaults are `match.json`'s, as for RESET ALL. A page reset saves like any edit but never repairs a blocked save.

### Players page

- PLAYERS lists each team's players (six alias rows per team) with their Steam account and an alias box (up to 16 characters; blank shows the game name), sorted by account (players without one after, in top-bar order). Opening the editor learns each player's account from the stock PLAYERS list: it briefly opens and closes each unknown player's profile card, like ShowRank ([mechanism](ENGINE.md#top-bar-cards)). Since 2026-10-24 (user: faster) it polls every 0.03 s instead of waiting fixed steps: the list is used as soon as it has rows (up to 1.5 s), a card the moment its account reads, and the next row starts at once. A row with no card (a bot) is given up after 5 times the slowest card seen so far, between 0.3 and 1 s (1 s until a card has been seen). While waiting it walks only the panels around the last card, with the whole UI every fifth look and on the last; an account already learned for another name is never pinned on a second one. In the VM, 10 players and 2 bots take 1.2 s (5 s before).
- An alias belongs to that account: it follows the player to any slot, survives restarts and saves with the match (`state.aliases`, `{ "<account id>": alias }`, at most 24). Bots (no account) get a session alias for their slot, kept in `dlf_player_names` (`{ amber/sapphire: [{ n, a } | null x6] }`) and shown only while the slot holds that game name. `dlf_accounts` holds `{ "<game name>": "<account id>" }`.
- The top bar's own name labels stay stock, since the debuff icons match by them.
- Where an alias shows (as Observer HUD, user 2026-10-24; not live-verified): the banner's name row; and, by in-game name, through the banner's map `{ "<in-game name>": alias }` on `dlf_aliases` (unique names only: a name two cards share gets none):
  - **World healthbars**: the stock `#name` (unit_status_overlay_v2.xml, bound to the overlay's dialog variable `name`) shows the alias. The map reaches every bar in `DLF_DEBUFF_CONFIG` (on change and every 4 s). The bar reads the in-game name from a hidden Label of its own bound to the same variable (`#DLFRealName`, 0x0, opacity 0, never collapsed), because writing `#name` unbinds it; status snapshots keep the in-game name, so the column still matches the card. A cleared alias, a bar recycled to another unit or a stopped script binds `#name` again. With the icons OFF the 4 s heartbeat still updates the name.
  - **Far-away hero markers** (stock `hud_unit_indicators_v2.xml`: `#unit_indicators_v2`, a `.PlayerEntry` per player, the name in `#PlayerName`'s Label): `dlf_names.js` writes the alias into the Label each 1 s tick, matched by the name it shows, else, for a name no card shows (a Steam friend nickname), by `#HeroIcon`'s `heroid` against the cards' `#HeroBadge` (two cards with that hero: none). The engine's text is kept and written back when the alias goes; a name the engine writes again is taken as the new real name.
  - **Kill feed rows** (stock `citadel_hud_data_feed_info.xml` in `#EventFeed`: `Label.killerInfo` / `.victimInfo` bound to `{s:killer_name}` / `{s:victim_name}`): resolved as the markers (by `#KillerImage` / `#VictimImage` for nicknames). With an alias the label gets it and a hidden bound Label of ours in the row keeps the in-game name, so a reused row shows its new names; without one the label is bound again. While there are aliases a 0.25 s watch picks up new rows. With no aliases neither the markers nor the feed are read.

## Save and share

- Edits save through the hosted `/dlf/` page and survive restarts ([ADR 0001](docs/adr/0001-save-through-hpv2-store-dlf-page.md)): hidden `CitadelHTMLPanel#DLFStore`, page `https://hantu-raya.github.io/hpv2-store/dlf/` (`D:/hpv2-store/dlf/index.html`, repo `Hantu-Raya/hpv2-store`), reply prefix `DLFS1:`, version 1, one key `hantu.dlf.v1/match`, ops `hello`, `r`, `w`, `d`, record `DLFS1.<fnv1a32>.<json with s:1>`. The page's ready reply advertises its record cap as `m` (4096); a page without `m` is the original 1024. The editor reads records up to 4096. When a record is over the page's cap it saves the same state without logo URLs (status SAVED - LOGO URLS THIS SESSION ONLY); a record over the cap even then is not written (TOO LONG TO SAVE).
- Phases: hello, read, then ready, blocked (damaged or newer save; only RESET overwrites) or unavailable (the session works without saving). Hello is re-sent every 2 s; from 30 s the status says OFFLINE - SESSION ONLY and it is re-sent every 5 s; after 3 minutes it gives up until the editor is opened again. A fragment-only URL never reloads a page whose load was lost, so the panel visits `about:blank` 6 s in and then at doubling gaps (12, 24, ... up to 60 s), and the next hello loads the page afresh (Observer HUD, live 2026-10-23; ported 2026-10-24). A late answer still loads the save unless an edit came first. Saves throttle at 1.5 s, one write in flight, retries 2/4/8 s. An edit made while the save loads wins over the stored record. DEV and graph settings live in the state, so they save with the match without a format change.
- The page must never touch the HP Colors or Buff Timer keys. A format change bumps `VERSION` in the page and `STORE_VERSION` in `dlf_menu.js` together.
- FORGET SAVE (SHARE / SAVE, THIS PC'S SAVE) sends op `d`: the page removes its one key (an additive op, no version bump) and the editor starts from the defaults with no player aliases or session names, so a code imported afterwards starts clean. It needs a second click within 3 s (the label reads CLICK AGAIN TO FORGET). Nothing is saved again until the next edit or import. Before the page is ready, or while a write is in flight, the delete waits (`forgetPending`) and the stored record is not adopted; an edit or import made while the delete is queued or in flight is saved after its reply (`saveAfterForget`; regression: forget, then import before the page has read, then restart). A page without `d` answers `invalid_request`: status FORGET NEEDS THE UPDATED SAVE PAGE and the save is kept. Offline it only resets the session. Status after a delete: SAVE FORGOTTEN - DEFAULTS.
- The original 1024-character page cannot hold long logo URLs: against such a page (or while Pages still serves it) the editor saves without them and shows SAVED - LOGO URLS THIS SESSION ONLY. The served page matched `D:/hpv2-store/dlf/index.html` (cap 4096, op `d`) on 2026-10-23 by `curl` and `diff`; the game's own CEF cache was not checked.
- SHARE SETTINGS: COPY CODE puts a `DLFC1.` code in the box and on the clipboard. The code is `DLFC1.<fnv1a of the JSON>.<base64url UTF-8 JSON>` (`$.DLFState.encodeShare`/`decodeShare`) with JSON `{ s: 1, state, n: <session names> }`: everything the editor saves (match details, team names, scores, bans, logos, banner style, soul graph, DEV debuff settings, player aliases by account) plus this session's names for players without a known account. IMPORT (or Enter) checks the code, replaces the settings with it through `normalize` and saves like an edit (it also clears a blocked store, like RESET); an empty box pastes from the clipboard first. A damaged or foreign code changes nothing (NOT A DLF CODE); a code from a newer version says so. Missing or out-of-range values take the defaults. Spaces and line breaks a chat client adds are ignored.
- Import keeps this PC's DEV settings (2026-10-24, Observer HUD's rule): a code still carries them (older codes stay valid), but IMPORT leaves debuff icons, the poll interval and STATUS LOG as they are on the importing PC. Everything else, the notice time included, comes from the code.

## Pickup rings

Canonical HPv2 top-bar buff rings, under DLF message names. Rings come only from heroes whose world healthbar the engine updates; a `LocalPlayer` card never shows rings; scanning waits until 5:00 on a normal map; with three or four buffs at once the row is wider than the 88px card. The build composition is in [design.md](design.md#pickup-rings).

## Ultimate cooldown

A seconds counter under each card's ultimate icon, copied from the game's `ult_cooldown` at 2 Hz, only on change. It shows only for a positive number, while the ultimate is unlocked and not ready, and never on the scoreboard. Cards are cached only while every cached child is valid, and cards without a name label are skipped.

## Urn carrier

User 2026-10-25: the urn carrier's card shows the stock minimap urn (`soul_jar_marker_psd`) beside the ult icon, the icon alone with a dark shadow (`#DLFUrn`, `.DLFUrnHeld`; a buff-style circle and border were dropped at the user's request, 2026-10-25). It is the last child of `#DLFUltColumn` with `ignore-parent-flow`, so the ult icon stays put (the first build put it in `#StatusRow`, which moved the ult icon 12 px left; user screenshot). It takes the first free buff slot right of the ult icon: the pickup rings put ceil(n/2) of n buffs left and floor(n/2) right in 24 px slots, so the urn goes to slot floor(n/2) + 1 (`.DLFUrnSlot1-3`, 23 / 47 / 71 px right of the ult icon's centre): beside the ult icon with 0 or 1 buff, one slot out with 2 or 3, two with 4 (past the 88 px card, like four rings). `dlf_urn.js` (top bar, 0.5 s) reads:

- Who: the kill feed's pickup row (`#EventFeed`, `.IdolPickedUp`, the picker as its killer; user screenshot `20261025070241_1.jpg` shows DIMOV in that row while Dimov, spectated, reads DELIVER THE URN). The card is the one whose `#HeroBadge` has the row's `#KillerImage` hero (the feed label may show a DLF alias); by the label's name only when the hero is unknown; none when two cards fit. A reused row counts again when its event, hero or name changes.
- How long: each minimap player marker carries `.PlayerIsHoldingIdol` while its hero holds the urn ([ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md#banner-and-top-bar)). Once that class has been seen, the icon goes when no marker has it (dropped or delivered), and a pickup it does not confirm within 2 s (an old row read at a late join) is dropped. Until it has been seen this session the feed alone decides. The minimap is read only while someone carries the urn.
- It also goes on a `.IdolReturned` or `.IdolExpired` row and at once when the carrier's card is `.Dead`. Joined mid-carry with no pickup row left, nothing shows (no guess). Classes are written only when the carrier changes. Not live-verified.

## Post-game board

When a game is finished, the post-game **Scoreboard** screen becomes Observer HUD's Night Shift board, ported ([ADR 0009](docs/adr/0009-post-game-board-ported-from-observer-hud.md); behaviour and live evidence: `observer_hud/SPEC.md` "Post-game board" and `observer_hud/ENGINE.md` "Post-game page").

- **Contents:** a header (team names, VICTORY / DEFEAT, net worth, kills, game time, match id) on torn paper; 12 player columns with exact stats (the best value in each row marked), the MVP / Key Player slip, souls by source and final items; a soul lead line with the lane objectives, each team's biggest lead and the final lead. Everything is read offline from the stock page (hidden bound labels per row, then the stock graph's tooltip column by column and the Lane Stats), so it works for any match the page opens, including `citadel_match_details <id>`.

A genuine duration lengthened past the threshold by items or upgrades also loses its number. Ends moving less than 0.4 s are not resent.
- Pulse (spec `pulse: true`, Celeste's Shining Wonder): the world sampler counts a hit when a status panel of the ability appears or its radial jumps back against its running direction by more than 20 deg (a refresh), and sends the bar's hit number as the item's sixth field; the slot's circle hops 5 px and swells to 130 % over 0.35 s (`.DLFPulse`) when that number changes, never on a heartbeat or a repaint. How the engine shows a re-hit is not seen live.
- A status that ends fades out over 1 s in its place (the CSS keyframe animation); then the others close up, and a status that returns shows at once. Images and classes are written only when a slot's ability changes.
- World healthbars show no stun icon, not even for one frame (stock shows none either); every other status stays stock. Shoulder Charge, item debuffs and unlisted abilities never reach the top bar. Icons exist only for heroes whose world healthbar the engine updates.

How the data moves:

- `dlf_debuff_world.js` (one per world healthbar) owns status sampling, stun-panel muting on hero bars and `DLF_DEBUFF_SNAPSHOT` publishing. `dlf_debuffs.js` (top bar) owns message validation, card matching, slot rendering, fades, pulses and `DLF_DEBUFF_CONFIG`. The key is the panel id minus `status_`, up to the first `/`; the two no-ability stun ids map to `citadel_ability_zip_line`. `RULES` has one entry per spec ability: `maxSeconds`, `buff`, `minStacks`, `ignore` (= `ignoreClasses`), `pulse`.
- Hero bars (`CLASS_PLAYER` on the world panel above the dialog, or on the dialog) scan only the direct children of `#StatusEffectContainer`, at the DEV poll interval (0.1-2 s, default 0.25 s); non-hero bars only re-check their class, backing off from 0.5 s to every 2 s (1-16 s until 2026-10-24: world panels are recycled, so a bar that became a hero after a long idle could stay unread for 16 s). A bar that stops being a hero sends one clearing message, gives its muted stun panels their styles back and drops its radial fits. One `Children()` read per sample; panels it just returned are not re-checked with `IsValid` in the same sample, cached parts (border, stacks, name) are. The countdown fit only takes samples at least 0.45 s apart (`FIT_GAP_MS`): closer whole-degree samples fail the 10% steadiness test.
- Snapshots are `{ source, instance, seq, at, name, items: [[key, debuff 0/1, count, end, stun 0/1(, hit)]] }` (at most 8 items; the sixth field only for pulse rules), sent on change, as one clearing message, and every 4 s while something shows. The top bar rejects stale `at`, replayed sequences, malformed items and keys outside `^[a-z0-9_]{1,64}$`, expires records after 10 s, and draws nothing for a name on two cards.
- `DLF_DEBUFF_CONFIG { on, poll, log, at }` goes out from the top bar on change and every 4 s; `pickup_host.js` routes it to `context.DLFOnDebuffConfig`, which restarts the bar's loop at once. OFF stops all world scanning (only the STATUS LOG still scans) and clears the cards.
- Card matching: `dlf_debuffs.js` rescans its cards every 5 s, and at once when a new or changed snapshot names a hero no card has (a newly spawned hero's card was missed by short statuses, user report 2026-10-20, cause traced with the STATUS LOG the same day: every first try logged `recv ... cards=0`). Rescans run at most every 0.5 s; a throttled or fruitless one is queued (one pending) for the deadline and retried while a record has been unmatched for under 2 s, so a second new hero or a card that appears just after its first snapshot is not dropped. Heartbeats of a matched record do not compare names (steady-state budget). In a real match every card exists from the start, so this mainly affected sandbox spawns and heroes joining late. The fix is not confirmed live.
- Card validity (2026-10-24): every tick `dlf_debuffs.js` also checks each card's first status slot, so a card whose slot panels were rebuilt (the engine rebuilds a card's children on a hero swap) is rescanned within a tick instead of at the 5 s rescan. One extra validity call per card per tick (debuffs budget 1195.5 to 1207.5 ops/s, ceiling 1250); rescanning every 2 s was tried and cost 1295.7.

### STATUS LOG (DEV)

Every hero bar prints its status list to the console on change, also with the icons OFF: `[DLF-DB] status + <NAME> status_<id> classes=[...] stacks=<n|-> radial=<deg|->`, `~` on a change of classes, stacks or visibility, `- <NAME> status_<id>` when gone; panels without a `status_` id print as `(no id)`. The top bar confirms the switch with `[DLF-DB] config icons=<bool> poll=<ms> log=<bool>` and each hero bar with `[DLF-DB] log on: <NAME>, <n> statuses`. Each snapshot is traced along its path: the hero bar prints `[DLF-DB] send <NAME> seq=<n> items=[...]` (`new relay` on that bar's first send); the top bar prints every snapshot it gets, as `[DLF-DB] recv <NAME> seq=<n> items=[...] cards=<n>` (`same` for an unchanged heartbeat; `cards=0`: no card has that name, `cards=2`: two cards share it and neither draws) or `[DLF-DB] drop <reason> <NAME> seq=<n>` (`malformed`, `clock at=... now=...`, `items`, `stale`, `unparsable`); and `[DLF-DB] show <NAME> <key> <icon>` when a slot is given a new icon. These are investigation leads, not a diagnosis: `show` only proves `SetImage`/`DLFShown` were called, not that the icon became visible, and a `send` with neither `recv` nor `drop` only says the top bar's listener never got it (relay, event routing or a stopped top-bar script). It covers other heroes' bars only, not the local player's own hero. Read it in VConsole (F8) or `citadel/console.log` with `-condebug`; leave it OFF for broadcasts.

## Spectator VData

`build_dlf.ps1` rebuilds `scripts/abilities.vdata_c` and `scripts/modifiers.vdata_c` from the installed game on every build and applies `spectator-status.json`, so world healthbars list the modifiers the top bar draws ([design.md](design.md#spectator-vdata) for the build). The spec:

- `abilities`: each entry names a hero, an ability key, its `modifiers` (by `_my_subclass_name`), `buff`, `stun`, `minStacks`, `ignoreClasses` and `maxSeconds`. Listed modifiers get `m_bIsHidden false`, `m_bIsHiddenOverhead false` and `m_eDrawOverheadStatus OVERHEAD_DRAW_FOR_EVERYONE`.
- `genericModifiers`: `modifier_citadel_stunned`, `modifier_citadel_stunned_no_cc_diminish` and `modifier_citadel_knockdown`, which make every ability's stun readable. Only the top bar filters them.
- `classSwaps`: Bebop's hook target gets the client class `modifier_citadel_link` ([ADR 0002](docs/adr/0002-spectator-vdata-class-swap-for-bebop-hook.md)).
- `unnamed`: modifiers with no English name. Unhidden, the watched hero's own HUD printed the raw token (`#Citadel_Ability_Hook/modifier_citadel_hooktarget`, live 2026-10-22), so they also get `m_eHudDisplayLocation DISPLAY_HUD_NONE` and keep their healthbar status. Currently Bebop's hook, the Doorman's Hotel Guest, Kelvin's Frozen Shelter debuff, Lady Geist's swap debuff, Sinclair's hex curse, Wraith's lift silence and Yamato's Flying Strike target. `spectator_status.py --localization <dir>` (passed by the build) fails when a listed modifier without a name in the installed English localization is not in `unnamed`, and when an `unnamed` entry is not a listed modifier. The 2026-10-23 fix is not seen live.
- Every record and marker must exist exactly once, so a rename fails the build.
