See enemy health instantly with colors that mean something. HP Colors turns the default health bar into a readable combat tool — color-coded by HP percentage, with ally bars, kill zone markers, and a full settings panel you can adjust mid-match without restarting.

**What it does**
Enemy health bars change color based on remaining HP. At a glance you know who is low, who is full, and who is about to die. The mod works with the new unit status HUD and gives you a live settings menu to tweak every color, threshold, and effect while you play.

**Key features**
- **Enemy HP coloring** — Three customizable zones (low / mid / high) with hex color pickers
- **Team color mode** — High HP shows your enemy's team color (amber or blue) instead of a generic green
- **Kill zone marker** — A visual bar appears when enemy HP drops below your chosen threshold
- **BPM pulse system** — Low-HP enemies pulse at a speed and intensity you control
- **HP counter** — Show current / max HP or percentage, with adjustable size and position
- **Ally bars** — Color friendly units too, with separate colors and pulse settings
- **Level indicator** — Tier-based border colors show enemy power level (Tier 2-5)
- **In-game settings** — Full Anita UI panel with live preview; no restart needed
- **Save and share** — Copy your config as a token and paste it back later

**What's new in v3**
This is a full rewrite. The old hardcoded colors are gone. Every setting is now exposed in the settings panel, including thresholds, pulse speed, text colors, and ally bar behavior. Settings persist across game sessions.

**Installation note**
Add `"citadel_unit_status_use_new" "1"` to your `gameinfo.gi` or autoexec. This is required because the command is cheat-locked in current builds.
