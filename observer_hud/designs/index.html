Community observer top bar: pick one

A tournament top bar that looks like part of Deadlock itself. Every design shows the same mock match: event, stage, game 3 of a Bo3, two teams with logos, twelve players with health, ultimate state and one dead player each. All art and fonts come from the installed game. Click a preview to open it full size (1920×1080, scales to your window); add ?long=1 to the address to see long team names. v1 shipped design 11. v2 ships two looks the observer picks in the editor: 14 · Base Game+ Names (default) and 12 · Night Desk.

Round 5 · Objective cues

Small chips under the clock that appear only while an objective is close and disappear after: bridge buff (last 30 s), mid-boss (last 60 s), rift (last 20 s, from its minimap warning), urn (last 60 s), and a held rejuvenator for its 3:00 buff in the holder's colour. The chips that show form one group centred under the clock, so the row is always symmetric; the mid-boss / rejuvenator chip (the mid objective) goes in the middle of the group. All chips are the same size (icon over time) and four fit between the team panels. Icon and time only, amber in the last 10 s, teal when up, no blinking. Each page shows a state sheet at the bottom.

Game rules

From the game files and server binary (decisions/objective-timers.md): bridge buff 5:00 then every 5:00; urn 10:00 then every 5:00; mid-boss respawns 7:00 after a kill, a minute less each time, at least 5:00 (the stock timer has it exactly); rift 12:00 then every 7:00, each within ±1:00 at random.

Rift

The rift's time is random and the server never sends it, so no honest countdown exists before its warning. The chip appears with the minimap warning, which a live test showed 20 s before it opens (12:38 for 12:58, 18:41 for 19:01), and counts down exactly.

Icons

Rift and urn use the game's colour images, the ones the stock kill feed shows for those events. Bridge and mid-boss use the stock modifier icons.

Round 4 · v2, after the broadcast bars

Taken from the Night Shift and Death Slam bars: a header band above the stock top bar (which moves down), the player names in a row over the cards instead of on the portraits, the organiser's wordmark or logo in the centre, and room for 28-character team names. Night Shift's PAUSED badge is the stock card's PauseIndicator, and the stock bar has its own midboss timer (#RejuvenatorTimer); v2 keeps both. Press Esc for the editor mock with the new broadcast fields.

Same stock bar

Design 11's stock rebuild, unchanged, moved down by the band. In game the band would be one more injected layout and the bar's move one inline margin.

New fields

Organiser name or logo URL for the centre; on 12 an optional info card (title, value, caption), for a prize pool or a sponsor line.

Long names

12 and 13 show 28-character team names in full; 12 also has the soul lead graph (pins every 5:00, shown at each mark). Player names over about 11 characters are cut.

Round 3 · Base game + tournament

Hairline (8) is kept. This one leaves Deadlock's own top bar as it is and adds only what a tournament needs. The stock bar is rebuilt from the game's citadel_hud_top_bar layout and CSS: card sizes, the soul tags, slanted health bars, ultimate icons, clock and team soul plates use the stock values and images. Added: team name plates with logo and series score, hero bans, GRAND FINAL with a small BO3, the event title, and player aliases on the portraits. Press Esc on the page (or "Tournament setup") for a mock of the in-game editor.

From the game files

Sizes, fonts and colours come from the stock CSS; the team colours are sampled from a live frame, because the engine sets them. The ultimate indicator is the stock default.

Editor mock

Event, stage, format, team names, tags, logo URLs (http/https only, monogram if it fails), series score, two bans per team, twelve player aliases. Edits show on the bar at once.

Long team names

Shrink to 12 px, then get cut off; 28-character names need a shorter display name.
