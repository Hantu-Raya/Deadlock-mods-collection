Observer HUD is a spectator HUD for community Deadlock tournaments. Under each player on the top bar it shows the crowd control on them with the seconds left, which a spectator normally can't see. It also shows purchases, ultimate cooldowns and objective timers, and puts your event, teams, logos, bans and series score on the stock top bar. You set it all up in game: on the private lobby page between maps, or from the Escape menu while you spectate. After the match, the post-game Scoreboard screen becomes a stats board for all 12 players with a soul lead graph.

For observer PCs only. The pak includes modified ability data so the status column works in spectator mode. Don't play matches with it installed: take it out of the addons folder before you queue.

Status column
  • Up to six status icons under every top-bar portrait, two per row, each with its seconds left.
  • Each icon has the colour of the team that applied it. Stuns get a stun mark.
  • Covers 34 abilities from 32 heroes (list further down), plus getting knocked off a zipline.
  • Djinn's Mark shows from 8 stacks. Celeste's Shining Wonder pulses once each time the orb bounces on that hero.
Ultimate cooldowns
The seconds left on each player's ultimate, right under its icon on the top bar. Nothing shows while it's ready.

Urn carrier
Whoever is carrying the Soul Urn gets the urn icon next to their ultimate icon on the top bar. It goes away when the urn is delivered, dropped or expires, or when the carrier dies. If you start watching while someone already has the urn, it shows from the next pickup.

Purchase notices (original feature by bytenode, rewritten for this mod)
  • When a player buys an item, a small item card with its icon and name pops up under their portrait.
  • Newest on top, three at most. Each one stays for 3, 5, 8 or 15 seconds (5 by default), or you can turn them off.
  • Joining a game halfway or skipping through a demo doesn't flood the bar with old purchases.
  • Item icons work with the English shop names. In other game languages the notice shows the name only.
  • Rewritten from the version in the DLF Observer Mod. It reads the game's own recent-purchases list instead of replacing the shop layout, so shop mods can stay installed. While you're not spectating it only checks every 2 seconds whether you are; while you spectate it checks the list twice a second.
Objective timers
  • Small chips under the clock that only show when an objective is close: bridge buff, urn, rift, mid-boss respawn, and the time left on a held rejuvenator in the holder's team colour.
  • The rift counts down from its 20 second warning, then shows LIVE while it's open. The game doesn't send the rift time any earlier.
  • Per timer, choose how early it shows: 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds, always, or off. The rift is just on or off.
  • The stock objective health bars and rift meter move under the timers so nothing overlaps.
Soul lead graph
Amber's soul lead over Sapphire across the match, with a pin every 5 minutes (or 1, 2, 3 or 10). By default it pops up for 30 seconds at each new pin. You can keep it on or turn it off, and change its size and position. The history starts when you start watching.

Two top bar looks
  • Base Game+ Names (default) looks like the stock bar. Each team gets a plate with its name, logo and a stage/format line, and the player names sit in a row above the cards. The organiser logo or name sits in the centre between the series wins, and each team's bans (two or three) sit beside the clock.
  • Night Desk is a dark band across the top. Event, stage and format sit on the left, game number and "first to" on the right. Each team gets its name, player names and bans, the logos sit in an arch by the centre with the series score, and you can add an info card (for example a prize pool).
Player aliases
Give players a display name. Aliases follow the player's Steam account, so they stick when someone changes their in-game name. While you spectate, the alias replaces their in-game name in the top bar's names row, on their healthbar, on the off-screen hero markers and in the kill feed, and the post-game board shows it too. Aliases made in 0.1.0 still work by name: click BIND TO ACCOUNT next to one on the PLAYERS page to move it to the account.

Series score
Set the wins by hand. When the end screen of a game you're spectating names a winner, that team gets +1 automatically (once per map, never past what the format needs).

Post-game stats board
  • Open a match's post-game page and pick the Scoreboard screen. It becomes one board for all 12 players: exact souls, kills, deaths, assists, player damage, objective damage and healing, with the best value in each row marked.
  • Souls by source for each player, their final items (hover one for its tooltip) and the MVP / Key Player slips.
  • A soul lead line across the whole match, with the lane objectives marked where they fell, each team's biggest lead and the final lead.
  • If you spectated that match with your setup, the header shows your team names, logos and organiser instead of the lobby names. They stay tied to that match for 2 days, also after a restart.
  • It works for any match the post-game page can open, not only the ones you spectated. Everything is read from the game's own post-game page.
Private lobby setup
The Escape menu doesn't open in a private lobby, so the lobby page carries what you change between maps.
  • Each team title shows that side's name, tag, logo, bans and series wins. Click the name or the pencil to edit the name, tag and logo link. Click a ban to pick the hero from a grid (a hero can only be banned once). Click a series dot to set the wins.
  • Click a player's name to give them an alias. It's kept by their Steam account.
  • An Observer HUD section under the lobby settings has the format (best of 1, 3, 5 or 7), the stage, and buttons to swap the HUD's sides, clear the bans and open SHARE.
  • SHARE copies your whole setup as one code, imports a code, or resets everything (RESET ALL asks twice).
  • When last match's players come back on the other sides, the lobby offers to swap the HUD's sides. It never swaps on its own.
  • These edits change the HUD on this PC only, not on other spectators' PCs.
Setup
  • In a private lobby, use the lobby page (above). While you spectate, open the Escape menu and click TOURNAMENT SETUP. The button only shows while you're spectating.
  • Pages: MATCH, TEAMS, PLAYERS, TOP BAR, NIGHT DESK, TIMERS, SHARE. Changes apply as you type.
  • Logos: paste an image URL. PNG, JPG and GIF work, and so do imgur, Giphy and Tenor links.
  • On TEAMS, a USE <TEAM>? button shows up when the players on a side match a team from your earlier matches. One click fills in its name, tag and logo.
  • Your setup is saved on this PC and comes back after a restart. A setup saved with 0.1.0 is copied over the first time this version starts. SHARE gives you one code that copies the whole setup onto another observer PC, however long the names and logo links are.
  • Saving goes through a small page on hantu-raya.github.io, loaded in Steam's built-in browser. If the page can't be reached, the editor says OFFLINE - SESSION ONLY and your settings last until you close the game.
Also changed while spectating
  • The SPECTATING name and the camera key hints are hidden. The keys still work.
  • The pause screen becomes a small II box that shows the unpause countdown.
  • The kill feed moves down when it would cover the first player's statuses.
Install
  • Extract pak10_dir.vpk from the archive into ...\steamapps\common\Deadlock\game\citadel\addons (create the addons folder if it's missing).
  • Your game\citadel\gameinfo.gi must load the addons folder (the line Game citadel/addons above Game citadel). If your other GameBanana mods work, it already does.
  • Fully restart Deadlock, spectate a match or play a demo, then open Escape and click TOURNAMENT SETUP.
  • If another mod already uses pak10_dir.vpk, rename this one to a free number.
Compatibility and known issues
  • It doesn't replace the top bar, Escape menu or healthbar layouts, so mods that do (HP Colors, ShowRank, QOL Lock, Top Bar Plus) can stay installed.
  • Don't use it together with the DLF Observer Mod. Both draw a band over the top bar.
  • The post-game board replaces the post-game page layout (citadel_db_page_post_game_new.xml). Another mod that replaces that file conflicts with it. ShowRank Barebones was installed during testing and both worked.
  • The lobby setup replaces the private lobby page layout (citadel_db_page_private_lobby.xml). Another mod that replaces that file conflicts with it.
  • Mods that ship their own ability data (abilities.vdata_c or modifiers.vdata_c) in a lower-numbered pak take over those files. The status column then shows only what the game itself draws.
  • When Bebop hooks someone, the hooked player isn't pulled toward Bebop on the observer's screen. It's a side effect of making the hook show in the column. Players in the match aren't affected.
  • Ability data changes with Deadlock patches. I update the mod after each patch; until then, some statuses may be missing.
  • Tested at 1920x1080 and 1600x900. Ultrawide and 4:3 haven't been tested yet.
