# Map: Observer HUD

## Destination

An observer-PC mod for community tournaments (pak10): two top bar looks, an Escape-menu editor, objective cues and the ability status column, injected beside the other HUD mods. Behaviour is in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md). Publishing needs the user's go-ahead.

- Start with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for rules and commands, [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) for vocabulary and [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) for behaviour.
