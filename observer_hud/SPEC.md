# Observer HUD spec

Status: v2 is built and installed as pak10 (`3e28f4fe`, 2026-10-23). The behaviour below is what the current source does; [MAP.md](MAP.md) says which parts were seen live. Terms are in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md); each engine fact carries its evidence label in [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md). Design history is in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md); mockups are in `designs/` (not shipped).

## Purpose

An observer-PC mod for community tournaments. The reason it exists (user, 2026-10-22) is the [ability status column](#ability-status-column): debuffs, stuns and pulls with their seconds, which a spectator cannot otherwise see. Around it the mod dresses the stock spectator top bar with an organiser's event name, stage, team names, logos, series score and bans, and offers an Escape-menu editor.

It is not DLF: DLF carries one league's branding. Fonts (Reaver, Retail Demo, VALVE Oracle) ship with Deadlock, so nothing is installed beside the pak.

## Looks

The editor's LOOK row picks the look (`state.style`). Both looks keep the stock top bar, the status column and the objective cues. v1's Base Game+ look (portrait aliases, event under the stage) is gone ([ADR 0005](docs/adr/0005-two-looks-v1-look-dropped.md)).

### Base Game+ Names

The default (`"names"`). The band is 26 px. Rev 2 from mockup `designs/d17-base-names-rev2.html` (user 2026-10-23, after a debate with a second model):

- A names strip over each team's cards, and the names row inside it (alias or stock name).
- The centre tab: the organiser logo, else the organiser name, else the event title, between a team-coloured series digit at each end (`#OHScoreLeft/Right`, 22 px, the tab's first and last children over its full height with `ignoreParentFlow`, live 2026-10-23: placed beside the tab they drifted). The name fits the 148 px between the digits (15, 13, 11 px, then ellipsis).
- A [plate](#placement) per team in the band at the screen's top edge, joined to its team's strip with no gap: the team name over a caption line (left plate: stage · `BOn`, or `BEST OF n`; right plate: `GAME n · FIRST TO m`) and the logo at the inner end beside the cards. The logo takes `logo.shape` and `logo.size` (24-50 px, the shared setting); a team image fills it (cover). The plate's height follows the logo both ways: logo + 13 px, 37 px at 24, 43 px at the default 30, 63 px at 50 (user 2026-10-23). A circle logo curves the plate's inner bottom corner by the height it hangs below the strip (11-37 px, inline `borderRadius` per corner); a square logo keeps the step. A 40 px join (`#OHJoinLeft/Right`) over the strip's start fades the plate's team colour into the strip. Narrow ends drop the caption (< 230 px), then shrink the name (< 210), then the logo (< 160); below 96 px the plate and its join go.
- Each team's two bans beside the stock clock box (clock bans): 32 px circles (28 px until 2026-10-23, user: "bigger a bit"), centred on the clock label. Where the bans would reach a team's innermost card (live 2026-10-23, 1600x900: they covered the innermost portraits), the stock teams spread outward through `#TeamsContainer`'s width (as Night Desk's logo spread) by what the tighter side lacks for its bans plus 4 px (`bansSpread`). The gaps are measured from the card spans while nothing is spread, so the spread never feeds back on itself; a HUD width change re-measures them unspread. No bans, no spread.
- No stage box: the [objective cues](#objective-cues) sit at Night Desk's spot under the soul plates (52 px under the clock). The rift chip hides while the stock rift HUD shows (it is moved under the chips and says the same thing); Night Desk keeps `LIVE`.
- The [soul lead graph](#soul-lead-graph) under the cues, as in Night Desk (design 19, user 2026-10-23).

### Night Desk

The second look (`"desk"`). The band is 56 px lacquer with one brass hairline.

- Left corner: event and "stage · BOn" (`BEST OF n` when the stage is empty). Right corner: `GAME n` and `FIRST TO m`. The game number is `min(bestOf, series sum + 1)`; `m` is the wins the format needs. Corners run from `EDGE` to the team blocks and hide below 96 px.
- Per team, a block over the stock team: the team name at its inner end over the player names and a `BANS` label with two bans (28 px circles, 24 px until 2026-10-23, user: "bigger a bit"; y 4-32 in the band's top row) at the outer end. The team's logo ring (30 px, monogram until a logo loads, team-coloured) sits on the centre plate's edge in an arch that bulges out of the plate in the band's top row (y 1-35), so the logos sit at the middle and the names row stays readable (mockup `designs/d15-night-desk-arch.html`, arch A, user 2026-10-23). The name ends 24 px from the team's inner end (`DESK_NAME`). Team names over 20 characters step down to 18 px, over 24 to 15 px. An event title over 16 characters steps down to 10 px.
- Team logo (design 16, user 2026-10-23): `logo.shape` circle or square, `logo.size` 24-50 px (30 default), `logo.gap` 4-48 px (12 default) between the logo's tab and the team name. The tab on each plate edge is the logo plus 2 px each way from the band's y 1 (`layoutArch` writes every size, radius and margin of `#OHArch`/`#OHRings`); a team image fills the logo inside its 1 px ring with the logo's radius. Past 30 px the stock `#TeamsContainer` gets `2 x 1.5 x (size - 30)` px wider than its measured stock width, so both stock teams (left- and right-aligned in it), their cards, names row, plates and team blocks move outward by `1.5 x (size - 30)`; in Base Game+ Names the width follows the clock bans instead (stock without them) and returns to stock outside spectating. Editor: TEAMS page, Team logos card, `#OHLogoCircle`/`#OHLogoSquare`, `#OHLogo{Size,Gap}{Down,Value,Up}` (the gap row is tagged Night Desk only and dims under Base Game+ Names). The centre plate grows `(size - 30) / 2` px a side (live 2026-10-23: a 44 px logo covered the organiser on the 192 px plate; when the plate took the whole spread, 50 px logos covered the innermost player names), so the organiser name or logo keeps 154 px between the tabs at every size and the innermost player names clear the tabs (at 50 px the name box ends at the tab's outer edge) and the name steps down 15, 13, 11 px per tick while wider, then ellipsis. Team images use `stretch-to-cover-preserve-aspect` so they fill the circle or square.
- The names row, the organiser logo or name over the series score (`n - n` in team colours), and an optional info card.
- The [soul lead graph](#soul-lead-graph) under the clock, with the cues above it. The stage box and clock bans are hidden.

## State

```text
{ v: 1, style ("names"|"desk"), event, stage, bestOf (1|3|5|7),
  amber/sapphire: { name, tag, logo: { url }, series, bans: [heroId, heroId] },
  org: { name, logo: { url } }, info: { title, value, caption },
  graph: { show (0|15|30|60|-1 s), every (60|120|180|300|600 s), size (70-150 step 10 %), x (-600..600 step 10 px), y (-40..300 step 5 px) },
  logo: { shape (circle|square), size (24-50 step 2 px), gap (4-48 step 2 px) },
  cues: { bridge, urn, rift, mid },
  buys (0|3|5|8|15 s),
  players: { "<Steam account>": { a: alias, n: last seen in-game name } },
  legacy: { "<in-game name>": alias }, pending: { "<in-game name>": alias },
  dev: { debuffs, debuffPollMs (100|250|500|1000|2000), logStatus } }
```

- Limits: event 40, stage 28, team name 28, tag 4 (upper-cased), alias 24, in-game name key 32, at most 24 players, 24 legacy and 24 pending aliases, organiser 24, info title 24 / value 16 / caption 32.
- Every field falls back to its default on its own when missing or invalid. Text that is too long is rejected, never cut. `$.OHState.normalize` is the only gate, so a value the editor accepts is the value the bar shows.
- Series runs from 0 to the wins the format needs; a shorter format caps it. When the stock match-end screen of a spectated game names a winner, that team's series goes up by one, once per map load and never past the wins the format needs; an abandoned game adds none (user 2026-10-23; [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md#objective-data) has the class mapping). The screen counts only after the map's bar has seen `#MatchEnd` without `ShowMatchEnd` while spectating: `#Hud` survives a map change, so a screen still carrying the last result adds nothing on the next map (it used to add a second win and block that map's own result) and nothing after a layout reload. A map whose first sight is its end screen is not counted; the console says so once (`match end seen without the game before it`). The editor's MATCH page corrects it. A hero is banned once: a repeat in a team's second slot is dropped, and a hero banned by both teams keeps only the Amber ban. A cleared team name shows the side's name (AMBER, SAPPHIRE).
- Logo URLs must be absolute `http(s)` with a plain host, no credentials, spaces or backslashes, at most 512 characters; otherwise they are rejected and never logged. The URL is kept as typed; `$.OHState.logoSources` gives the images to load in order, each tried after the previous one fails (user 2026-10-23: "support imgur and more with gifs"). The game loads PNG, JPEG, still WebP and GIF; animated WebP draws grey bars and video does not load (DLF [ENGINE.md](../dlf/ENGINE.md)). So: imgur page links and `.gifv`/`.mp4`/`.webm`/`.webp` links load `i.imgur.com/<id>.gif`, then `.png`; Giphy page, embed, sticker and media links the media GIF; Tenor media links their full GIF (format code `AAAAC`), Tenor page links plus `.gif` (Tenor redirects; whether the game follows the redirect is not seen live); Discord media-proxy and fxtwitter WebP as GIF (DLF's rule); Discord CDN emoji, avatars and icons as GIF when animated, else PNG; X WebP as PNG; GitHub blob pages from `raw.githubusercontent.com`; Dropbox share pages with `raw=1`. Imgur albums and galleries need the API and stay unsupported.
- `graph.show` is how long the graph shows at each new pin (`-1` always, `0` off). `graph.every` is the mark interval. `graph.size` lays the graph out larger (box, fonts, line, pins; no bitmap scale) around its top centre, so size never moves it; `graph.x`/`graph.y` move it from its spot (centred in the clock stack, 4 px under the timers). Editor: TIMERS page, Soul lead graph card (both looks; size, X and Y on one row), `#OHGraph{Size,X,Y}{Down,Value,Up}`.
- `cues` values are the seconds before a spawn that the countdown shows (15, 30, 60, 120), `0` off, `-1` always. The rift is only `0` or `-1`.
- `buys` is how long each [purchase notice](#purchase-notices) shows (default 5 s, as DLF; `0` off). It travels in share codes.

### Sample defaults

A fresh state fills the boxes with real text (user, 2026-10-22): event `Community Cup Season 2`, stage `Grand Final`, `bestOf` 3, organiser `Community Cup`, teams `Night Owls` / `NOW` and `Brass Saints` / `BRS`, info card `Prize pool` / `$2,130` / `Donate to increase`, graph `show` 30 and `every` 300, cues `bridge` 30, `urn` 30, `rift` -1, `mid` 30. Logo URLs and aliases start empty. RESET returns to these values; an emptied box stays empty (except a team name). Most validator checks boot with a blank state; `samples: true` checks the shipped text.

### Share codes

`OHC1.<fnv1a>.<base64url>` of `{ s: 1, state }` without `dev`, at most 8192 characters. Import replaces the setup (missing fields take defaults) and keeps this PC's DEV settings. A corrupt code and a code from a newer version are told apart.

## Host and injection

The pak overrides only `hud_spectate_bar.xml` (stock plus an `oh_host.css` include and the scripts), `citadel_status_effect.xml` and the two VData files ([ADR 0001](docs/adr/0001-inject-from-hud-spectate-bar.md)). The host script loads at UI start and loads the mod's layouts with `BLoadLayout` into panels it creates:

| Panel (id) | Layout | Goes into |
|---|---|---|
| `OHBandHost` | `observer_hud/band.xml` | the parent of `#TopBar`, right after it |
| `OHBar` | `observer_hud/tournament_bar.xml` | `#TopBar` |
| `OHDebuffsHost` | `observer_hud/debuffs.xml` | each `CitadelHudTopBarPlayer` card |
| `OHMenuHost` | `observer_hud/editor.xml` | `#EscapeMenu` |
| `OHMenuButton` | none (a native `Button`) | `#SubOptions`, after `.FeedbackRow` |

- State: `$.OHHost` owns it. The editor changes it only through `setState`; readers take `getState()` and compare `version()` or use `onChange`. The state is also kept as JSON on the HUD root attribute `observer_hud_state`, which a layout reload adopts (`carried()` is then true).
- A 1 s tick re-injects what is missing: `#TopBar` and the cards are rebuilt on map loads and hero swaps ([ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md#host-and-injection)). A cached card is reused only while its cached children are valid. Panels of an earlier script instance are deleted once at boot. Until the script sits under `CitadelHudRoot` the tick retries with a backoff (0.5 s doubling to 8 s).
- Outside spectating (`spec_mode`, `TeamSpectator` or `replay_playback` on `CitadelHud#Hud`) the tick only checks those classes; the stylesheets gate the same way. When spectating ends, the stock top bar's inline `margin-top` and the collapsed `#RejuvenatorTimer` are cleared.
- A missing stock id (`#TeamFriendly`, `#GameTime`, `#EscapeMenu`, `#SubOptions`, a card's name label) hides only the part that needs it and is logged once.

## Placement

- The band spans the HUD width from the HUD's top-left corner. `#TopBar` is `width: fit-children` and clips, so the bar also sets its width to the HUD width. The host moves `#TopBar` down with an inline `margin-top` of the band height plus the stock 8 px, tucked 8 px up: 26 or 56 px (user 2026-10-23: with no tuck the portraits hung 20-26 px under the band, "not connected"; tucked 16 px, Celeste's horns and tall hair crossed it, "too up").
- The band panel itself is 220 px tall and transparent; its lacquer is a 56 px full-width child at about 82 % opacity with `world-blur: gaussian(2,2,2)`. The dark panels drawn over the world blur it (names strip and centre tab, info card, and the graph only while shown); panels drawn on the band do not ([ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md#panorama-styling-behaviour)).
- Everything is positioned from the stock panels it belongs to, measured with `GetPositionWithinWindow()` and `actualuiscale_x`: the team panels (`#TeamFriendly` Amber, `#TeamEnemy` Sapphire) and the `#GameTime` clock. A team's plate and block follow its stock panel when the sides swap.
- Names plates sit in the band at the top edge, at the outer edge of each team panel with no gap, no closer than `EDGE` (72 px) to the screen edge. Width is the free space up to 250 px. Below 230 px the caption goes, below 210 px the name drops to 14 px, below 160 px the logo goes, below 96 px the plate and its join hide.
- Clock bans hug the stock clock box (5 px padding plus a 6 px gap); the left team's start 36 px per ban further left (`BAN_STEP`: 32 px ban plus margins).
- The cue stack starts below the clock (and below the measured stock `#RejuvenatorCharges` while a rejuvenator is held and a side shows its icons).
- Names: one label per card (`OHWho0..11`, Amber's cards first), centred on its card. The stock cards are equal (`playerDetailsWidth`, margin 0) and flow from the left of `#PlayersContainer`, which is as wide as the team panel and wider than the cards, so dividing the panel evenly drifted the names (live 2026-10-23). Each tick measures each team's first card (one read per team) and centres card k at `first.x + (k + 0.5) x first.w`; without a measurable card the team panel is divided evenly.
- Write styles, texts, classes and hero ids only on change, checking the cache before the panel. Steady state makes no writes and no extra native calls.

## Logos

One `Image` per URL, created when the look shows that logo (a plate logo in Base Game+ Names, the desk ring in Night Desk, the organiser logo in both). It shows only after its own `ImageLoaded`. A failed or replaced image is deleted and the monogram (or wordmark) stays. URLs pass `cleanUrl` and are never logged. The editor shows a status note under each URL field.

## Aliases

Aliases v2 (plan phase 3, [decisions/postgame-identity-perf-plan.md](decisions/postgame-identity-perf-plan.md) decisions 2-3). A card's alias comes from, in order: `players` by the Steam account joined to the card, a `pending` alias by name, a v1 `legacy` alias by name. A name two cards share keeps the stock name on both (no account join for it either). Nothing is added to the portraits; the band's names row shows the alias, else the stock name. The editor's PLAYERS page lists up to six names per team from the roster and follows joins and swaps while it is open.

- **Accounts, passively.** Each far-away marker (`PlayerEntry`) carries a Steam account in `CitadelUserName#PlayerName.accountid` and a side class (`team_1` Amber, `team_2` Sapphire). The host reads them once a second while fewer than the cards minus one have an account, each entry until it has one (an entry goes from 0 to its account once, when its marker is first set; 6 of 12 were set 8 s into the demo, 11 about a minute later), then stops. Cost: about 6 us/s while scanning, nothing after. Nothing is read for an entry that has an account. Accounts are never logged, only counts (`[OH] accounts: 11 read from markers, 11 of 12 cards joined`). A map load (a new `#OHBar`) forgets them.
- **Join (account to card), per side.** First the marker label (the Steam persona) equals the card name, case-insensitive, unique on both ends; then elimination: one card and one account left. Two cards and two accounts left is no guess; a duplicate name, a persona that matches no card and a bot (account 0, never listed) stay unjoined. Hero ids cannot join: `CitadelHeroImage` exposes none to JS, so the old marker and kill-feed hero fallback is gone.
- **No hover and no click path (user decision 2026-10-25).** Player names are not clickable and the DLF PLAYERS-tab probe (opening profile cards) is not used. The hovered top-bar card fires `CitadelTopDownScoreboardPlayerHovered` on its own panel with no arguments (live probe, [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md#account-ids-and-hover)); no account, tooltip node or marker class follows from it in the HUD context. Which stock panel shows the account on a real hover is still open.
- **Editor.** Typing an alias for a card with a joined account writes `players[account] = { a, n }`. For a card without one (a bot, an unmatched persona, a duplicate name, no marker yet) it writes `pending[name]`: it shows at once by name, is never saved or shared, and binds to the account (`players`, `pending` entry removed) only if the account arrives in the map load it was typed in. After a map load it stays a session alias by name. A full `players` list (24) keeps the alias pending. Clearing the box removes it everywhere. The last-seen name `n` follows the card.
- **v1 migration.** A v1 `aliases` map (a save, a share code) lands in `legacy`; it keeps applying by name. A card with a `legacy` alias and a joined account shows the chip `BIND TO ACCOUNT` (`#OH{Side}Player{n}Bind`); one click moves it into `players`. Nothing is re-keyed without that click. The next local save writes the v2 shape (`players`, `legacy`; phase 4 moves it to its own store key).
- **Share codes** carry `players` and `legacy`, never `pending` or DEV. Local saves never carry `pending`.

While spectating the same aliases (unique names only, `$.OHHost.aliases()`) also replace the name on (user 2026-10-23, screenshot of the far-away markers):

- **World healthbars** (stock `unit_status_overlay_v2.xml` `#name`, bound to the overlay's dialog variable `name`). The aliases reach each world context in `OH_DEBUFF_CONFIG`: on change, every 4 s, and with `{}` once spectating ends. The world sampler reads the in-game name from a hidden Label of its own bound to the same variable (`#OHRealName`: 0x0, opacity 0, not collapsed), because writing `#name`'s text unbinds it. With an alias, `#name` shows it. Otherwise, and when the bar is recycled to another unit, stops being a hero bar or the script stops, `#name` gets `{s:name}` back. A recycled bar shows its new hero's name within 0.5 s. Status snapshots keep the in-game name, so the column still matches the card.
- **Far-away hero markers** (stock `hud_unit_indicators_v2.xml`: `CitadelHudUnitIndicatorsV2#unit_indicators_v2`, one `PlayerEntry` per player with the name Label inside `CitadelUserName#PlayerName`, the hero image and the distance). The host's 1 s tick writes the alias into that Label: matched through the entry's account when the join knows it (a Steam nickname works), else by the name it shows, and then only against a card of the marker's own side. The name the engine wrote is kept and written back when the alias goes or spectating ends; if the engine writes the name again, the alias goes back on next tick. With no aliases and none shown, the markers are not read at all. Stock styling stays: only the stock Label's text changes.
- **Kill feed rows** (stock `citadel_hud_data_feed_info.xml`, `CitadelHudInfoFeed` rows in `#EventFeed`: `Label.killerInfo` and `Label.victimInfo` bound to `{s:killer_name}` / `{s:victim_name}`; user 2026-10-23, screenshot `.../20261023202920_1.jpg`). Resolved by name only (the row's hero images carry no id to JS). With an alias the label's text becomes it, which unbinds it, so a hidden Label of ours in the row (0x0, opacity 0), bound to the same variable, keeps reading the in-game name; a row the engine reuses shows its new names. Without an alias the label is left alone, or bound again when an alias is cleared or spectating ends. While spectating with aliases a 0.25 s watch reads `#EventFeed`'s child count and passes over the rows when it moves (an alias shows within 0.25 s of a new row); the 1 s tick passes over them as well. Other feed rows (team events, bans) are not touched.

## Soul lead graph

Both looks (Night Desk only until design 19, user 2026-10-23). Each tick while spectating, the host reads `#GameTime` and the stock `TeamScoreFriendly` / `TeamScoreEnemy` `ScoreLabel` texts (Friendly is Amber, [seen live](ENGINE.md#top-bar-and-hud-geometry)) and keeps one sample per 5 s of game time. The lead is Amber minus Sapphire in thousands.

- A mark's pin is the first sample within 20 s after it. Marks fall every `graph.every` (1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 min; default 5:00). Changing the interval re-pins from the samples without showing the graph.
- A clock that runs back by more than 3 s (a seek) drops later samples. History starts when the observer starts watching.
- The drawn series is the run of consecutive pinned marks ending at the latest; it starts from 0:00 only when it reaches back to the first mark. It needs at least two pins. The line runs through the samples between pins as a monotone cubic (Fritsch-Carlson, no overshoot) over at most 24 averaged knots, drawn as 3 px segments over 2 px fill columns (amber above zero, sapphire below). Inner pins show only when 14 px apart.
- The graph shows for `graph.show` seconds after a new pin (always for `-1`). Panels are rebuilt only when a pin changes. The header shows the current lead as `<tag> +n.nk` in the leader's colour.
- It sits 4 px under the timers, or 4 px under the stock rift HUD or [objective health](#objective-health) moved there (its own Y still applies when that is lower). While the rift HUD and an objective bar both show it waits (hidden, `Graph.held`) instead of sitting a whole stock panel lower; a running show window starts over while it waits, so it shows its full time once either clears, and `-1` simply comes back (design 19 rev 2).

## Objective health

The stock objective health (`#objectives_health_friendly`, hud.xml `gameplay_hud`; hud.css 6813: margin-top 0, flow down, z-index 20) draws a tier-2 walker as its 100 x 50 image over a 16 px bar, its top 160 px under the panel's top (HUD y 160), both teams' walkers side by side, the mid-boss and patron in their own rows. At that spot the timers and the soul lead graph covered it (live 2026-10-23, both looks). While spectating, `placeObjectives` gives the panel an inline margin-top that puts the top of the highest shown bar (`.objective.is_active`, not `.is_tier1`, with a layout box) 4 px under the timers, or 4 px under the stock rift HUD while it shows; the bars keep all of the game's styling (designs 19/20). The margin comes from the panel's parent and the panel's stock top under it (measured before the first write), so a lagging layout pass cannot make it oscillate; the highest bar's offset under the panel's top is measured while one shows and kept (160 until then), so the margin stays set between bars and a new bar appears in place. The margin is cleared when spectating ends.

## Objective cues

`#OHCues` sits in the clock stack: under the stage box in Base Game+ Names, under the soul plates with the graph below in Night Desk. One chip per objective, shown (`.OHOn`) only while close. How close comes from `state.cues` (default 30 s; the rift only off or on). The editor's TIMERS page has an Objective timers card.

| Chip | Shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bridge buff | countdown in the setting's window before 5:00 and every 5:00; `UP` while the minimap has `powerup_spawn` markers, at most 30 s (always: until taken) | game clock; minimap |
| Urn | countdown before 10:00 and every 5:00; `UP` for 15 s after | game clock only |
| Rift | `mm:ss` from the minimap `koth_warning` (a 20 s countdown the host starts when it first sees the marker); `LIVE` while `#KothCashInMeter` is visible | minimap; stock capture meter |
| Mid-boss | countdown of the stock `#MidbossTimerLabel` while `#RejuvenatorTimer` has `midboss_cooldown`, in the setting's window, or the whole respawn right after a held rejuvenator; `UP` for 30 s after it ends (always: while alive) | stock timer |
| Held rejuvenator | the buff's time left in yellow (stock `goldColor` `#FFED79`) over the holder's bar (the team whose cards carry `HasRejuvenator`): 3:00 from the pickup when it was seen, else from the stock rejuvenator circle's radial (`#RejuvenatorTimer #StatusEffectsBorder` clip, 360 deg = 3:00, direction learned from two samples at about 2 deg/s); `HELD` until then or without a radial. When it ends the mid-boss respawn countdown follows until the mid-boss is back (user 2026-10-23) | stock classes, circle and label; cards |

- The last 10 s of a countdown add `OHSoon`. A held rejuvenator always shows, whatever the mid-boss setting; the respawn countdown after it shows unless the mid-boss is Off. In the live probe (2026-10-22) the stock label logged no change during the buff (6:54 at the 20:00 pickup, next 3:54 at 23:00), so the respawn time is shown only after it.
- Chips are four generic slots (`#OHCue0..3`, kind class `.OHKind*`) filled in order bridge, urn, rift, with the mid-boss chip inserted in the middle of whatever shows. The row is centred as a group, so it is always symmetric (user, 2026-10-22) and the mid-boss chip is exactly under the clock when one or three chips show ([ADR 0008](docs/adr/0008-objective-chips-only-when-close.md)).
- Chips are 46 px wide (icon over time). The mid-boss chip uses the stock rejuvenator icon and yellow `#FFCC00`; a held rejuvenator has a 3 px bottom bar in the holder's stock team colour (`#D6880C` Amber, `#4F74C3` Sapphire).
- The stock `#RejuvenatorCharges` stays visible. Its `#RejuvenatorTimer` progress circle is collapsed while spectating (user, 2026-10-22); its classes still drive the mid-boss chip.
- A seek back resets the trackers; a jump forward only forgets that a rejuvenator-free sample came first.

## Ability status column

DLF's top-bar column, ported under an `OH` prefix (`dlf/AGENTS.md` runtime rules apply). It has three parts.

1. **Spectator VData.** `scripts/abilities.vdata_c` and `modifiers.vdata_c` are rebuilt from the installed game on every build with `dlf/spectator-status.json` and `dlf/scripts/spectator_status.py` (shared with DLF), then verified after packing. 2. **World sampler** (`oh_debuff_world.js`, hosted in `citadel_status_effect.xml`, [ADR 0003](docs/adr/0003-world-sampler-in-citadel-status-effect.md)). It runs once per `CitadelStatusEffect` list; HUD lists (top panel `CitadelHudRoot`) stop at once. It sends changes plus a 4 s heartbeat as `OH_DEBUFF_SNAPSHOT` through the sibling relay. `OH_DEBUFF_CONFIG` reaches each world context's own listener and carries:
   - the DEV settings;
   - the aliases;
   - `watch`: spectating;
   - `wake`: a counter the column bumps when its cards change or a card loses the stock `.Dead` class (a respawn, checked once a second).

   `RULES` must equal `dlf/spectator-status.json`. Since 2026-10-25 (phase 1 of [the plan](decisions/postgame-identity-perf-plan.md)), the sampler works only when needed:
   - **Sleeping:** while not spectating (`watch: false`), or with the icons off and no alias, a bar clears what it sent, gives the stock name and stun panels back, and schedules nothing until a config message wakes it. The column sends the config every 4 s in both states, so a bar created while playing goes to sleep within 4 s.
   - **Non-hero bars** re-check their class with a 0.5 s doubling backoff. The cap is 2 s for 30 s after load, the start of spectating or a wake (the class lands up to about 15 s late), then 4 s.
   - **Hero bars** take a full sample (class, name, every status panel) on a change and at least every 2 s. In between, every `dev.debuffPollMs` they read only the status list's signature: the child count plus the last child's id. Live P5 (client 6819) found that every stun and listed status arrives as a new panel. On top of that:
     - every 0.5 s the shown statuses' radials and stacks, and the in-game name while an alias exists or a status shows;
     - at every poll for pulse and `minStacks` rules, whose refreshes and stacks keep the count.

     The DEV status log always takes full samples.
   - **Latency floors** (validator `column: latency floors`, worst of four phases): onset, stun, removal, stack threshold and refresh pulse within 0.3 s at the 0.25 s poll; a respawn within 1.6 s; spectating again within 1.5 s.
3. **Top-bar column** (`oh_debuffs.js`, injected per card from `debuffs.xml`). Six slots under each stock card, matched by player name: 34 px circles with a 22 px ability icon, the applying team's colour, a stun mark, and seconds or a stack badge. The seconds read one second longer than the predicted end (`END_PAD_MS`, user 2026-10-23). A removed status fades out over 1 s. Snapshots expire after 10 s. The cards come from the host's registry (`$.OHHost.cards()`, re-read when `cardsVersion()` moves; slots cached per column panel), names are read at most once a second, and a steady column repaints every 5 s so a name that binds late still matches. After a hero swap rebuilds a card, its statuses are back within 2 s (validator: every tick phase; it was up to 5.5 s). While not spectating the column does nothing (records are kept and expire); on return it rebuilds and paints, so a status that ended meanwhile is gone.
   Layout (user 2026-10-23): a centred grid two wide (`#OHDebuffGrid`). One status sits in the card's middle; a second moves the first 20 px left and pops in 20 px right, the pair symmetric about the middle; a third sits centred under the pair; a fourth moves the third under the first and pops in under the second; then a fifth centred under them and a sixth beside it (three rows, six at most). In display order a status keeps its place; when one ends it fades where it is for 1 s, then the later ones close up. Each status keeps its slot panel while it shows, so moves are glides (`.OHGlide`: 0.22 s transform transition, no layout) and only a new status pops in (the circle grows from 40 % past full size, 0.22 s); a slot's first placement is written before its glide class goes on (next frame), so it never slides in from an old place. Sizes fit the 88 px stock card: pair 74 px wide with 6 px between its circles, 14 px to the next card's pair, so each card's statuses read as a group. The grid is as tall as its rows (39 px each, `.OHRows1-3`), and the purchase notices follow under it.
4. **Pulse per hit** (spec `pulse: true`, Observer HUD only): Celeste's Shining Wonder (`ability_unicorn_dazzlingorb`, its slow `dazzling_orb_slow_modifier`, 1.5 s per hit) pulses its slot once each time the orb bounces on that hero (user 2026-10-23): the circle hops 5 px and swells to 130 % over 0.35 s (`.OHPulse`). The world sampler counts a hit when a status panel of the ability appears or its radial jumps back against its running direction by more than 20 deg (a refresh), and sends the bar's hit number as the item's sixth field; the column pulses when that number changes, never on a heartbeat or a repaint. Two hits inside one poll (0.25 s by default) pulse once. How the engine shows a re-hit (a new panel or a restarted radial) is not seen live; both count.

- The spec list decides which abilities show; a buff shows only where the spec says; `minStacks` applies (Djinn's Mark from 8 stacks); a stun is its own slot with a countdown fitted from three radial samples.
- World stun icons are hidden by `.WindowRoot.CLASS_PLAYER .statusEffect.stunned` in `oh_status_world.css`, plus an inline fallback. The selector stays short.
- pak10 loses the two VData files to any lower-numbered pak that ships them; the column then shows only what stock data draws.

## Local save

`oh_menu.js` keeps the whole state (DEV and `pending` aside) in Steam's CEF localStorage through the hpv2-store page `https://hantu-raya.github.io/hpv2-store/oh/v2/` (store v2; [ADR 0010](docs/adr/0010-store-v2-and-match-cards.md), [ADR 0004](docs/adr/0004-local-save-through-hpv2-store.md) for the transport). The v1 page `/oh/` stays untouched for released paks.

- Transport: hidden `CitadelHTMLPanel#OHStore` in `editor.xml`; request in the URL fragment, reply in `HTMLTitle` with prefix `OHS2:`, protocol version 2. Keys `hantu.observer_hud.v2/state` (the record `OHS1.<fnv1a32>.<{s:1,state}>`, cap `m` 8192; over it the logo URLs are dropped from the record and the status says so) and `hantu.observer_hud.v2/matches` ([Match cards](#match-cards)). The game cuts a title at 4096 characters, so the page never replies with more: the record is read in pieces (`r` with `s` = offset; each reply carries `n`, `s` and `v`) and fixes v1's bug that a record over about 4000 characters saved but never read back.
- Migration: when the v2 state key is empty the page returns v1's record (`f: 1`; v1's key is only read, never written, and 0.1.0 keeps working on it); the next save writes the v2 key.
- Phases: hello, read, then ready, or blocked (damaged or newer save; only RESET ALL overwrites), or unavailable. Hello is re-sent every 2 s; from 30 s the footer says `OFFLINE - SESSION ONLY` and it is re-sent every 5 s; after 3 minutes it gives up until the editor is opened again. A fragment-only URL never reloads a page whose load was lost (an error page), so the panel visits `about:blank` 6 s in and then at doubling gaps (12, 24, ... up to 60 s), and the next hello loads the page afresh. A late answer still loads the save unless an edit came first. Saves throttle at 1.5 s, one request in flight, retries at 2, 4 and 8 s. Status shows in the footer `#OHSaveStatus`.
- The record is adopted only by a fresh process (`$.OHHost.carried()` false) and only if no edit came first. The page never touches the HP Colors, Buff Timer, DLF or v1 keys.

## Match cards

Plan phase 4 ([ADR 0010](docs/adr/0010-store-v2-and-match-cards.md)). A card names the two teams of one match by the Steam accounts of their players: `{ a, b, t, r, w, id, at }` = the accounts of the Amber and Sapphire sides (1-6 numbers each, no repeats), each team's `[name, tag, logo URL]`, the organiser `[name, logo URL]` or 0, the winner (0, 1 Amber, 2 Sapphire), an optional match id and the time in seconds (the newest card wins). At most 2400 characters.

- **Written by the HUD** when both sides have at least 5 known accounts (the marker accounts, [Aliases](#aliases)), again when the account set grows (11 then 12), again whenever a team or the organiser changes while the match is live (user 2026-10-26: a logo set during the match must reach the post-game header), and at match end with the winner (the stock `#MatchEnd` screen, once per map, only on a bar that saw the game running). After match end the card is the ended match's: setting up the next teams does not change it. An untouched sample name (SPEC "Sample defaults", no logo) is left out, and with nothing to show there is no card. A new map load starts over. The HUD cannot read the match id, so its cards have none.
- **Reaches the board two ways.** In this process: `OHM1:<json>` on `ClientUI_FireOutput` (live P3), sent again every 5 s for as long as the HUD has the card, spectating or not, because the board's script only runs once the post-game page has shown. For later: the editor's store panel upserts it (`u`, one request in flight after the setup record's, retried at 2 and 4 s, then dropped).
- **Matching.** A card fits a match when it shares at least 10 of the match's 12 accounts. The side comes from the players (a card whose Amber team sits on the board's team 2 swaps). A card with a match id answers only that match; an id-less card answers any. Several cards: one for the exact id (the newest of them), then the one that shares most, then the newest. Newer replaces older for the same roster; an older card never replaces a newer one.
- **Tied to the match id, kept 2 days** (user 2026-10-26). The board ties every fitting id-less card to the match it shows: a copy with the match id is kept for the session and upserted once (`u`); in the store it replaces the id-less card of the same players written no later than it (a newer id-less card, their next map, stays). After a restart the match is found by its id and another match of the same players never borrows its teams. A card is ignored 2 days after its `at` (board and page) and the page deletes it from the key on the next request that reads it; an expired card is not stored.
- **Untrusted input.** Anything on `ClientUI_FireOutput` may be forged. `$.OHState.cleanCard` refuses bad shapes (not 2 teams, ids that are not accounts, repeats, over 6 a side, over 2400 characters) and empties names that are too long and URLs that are not plain http(s); the store page cleans again.
- **Learned registry (phase 5).** The page learns team -> its 12 most recent accounts from cards of ended matches (a winner is set; an empty name, i.e. an untouched sample, teaches nothing; the same name in any case is one team). `s` answers per side only when at least 4 of its accounts are members of one team and at most 2 of another's. It is only ever offered: the editor's TEAMS page shows `USE <TEAM>?` (`#OH{Side}UseTeam`, applied by the click: name, tag, logo). The board has no team editing and no suggestion (user 2026-10-26: the teams come from the setup used during the match).
- **Store page ops (v2):** `hello` (`ready` with `v`, `m`, `h`), `r`/`w` (the setup record), `u` (upsert a card: read, merge and write in one request, so two panels cannot lose each other; a card with the same roster and match id replaces the old one), `q` (the card for up to 12 accounts and an optional id; one card, under the cap). A card for a match id also replaces the id-less card of its players written no later than it; cards older than 2 days are deleted by the next request that reads the key. `matches` is `{ v: 2, T: [[name, tag, logo], ...], m: [{ a, b, t: [i, j], o, w, id?, at }] }`: teams and the organiser listed once, about 180 characters a match, at most 32768 characters; the oldest cards go first, then teams no card uses.

## Budgets and logging

- Validator budgets are weighted: microseconds of native time per second, each counted call priced at its live per-call cost (P0 probe, client 6819: `IsValid` 50 ns, `BHasClass` 400, `GetChildCount` 200, `Children()` 350 plus 140 per child, `$.Schedule` 1000, ...; table `WEIGHT_NS`). Raw calls are printed beside them. The live-like scenario has:
  - 12 hero bars at the 0.25 s poll, each with a listed and an unlisted status and `CLASS_PLAYER` on the world panel;
  - 60 idle bars, measured after their 30 s wake window;
  - 12 far-away markers and the stock kill feed;
  - the host with band, names (one alias), graph sampling, column and objective health placement.

  Budgets: 222 spectating, 280 while every card counts down (the column's 0.25 s tick), 6 when playing with pak10 installed (world bars and statuses present), 6 after spectating, 6 never spectated. There are zero text, style and class writes in steady state. `OH_ATTRIB=1` prints the weighted cost per script function. The post-game board has its own budget:

- Own panels are hidden by class (`OHGone`, `OHPlateGone`), never by an inline `visibility: collapse` later cleared with null: live, a cleared inline collapse leaves the panel hidden ([ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md)).
